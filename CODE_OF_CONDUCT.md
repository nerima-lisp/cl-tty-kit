# Code of Conduct

`cl-tty-kit` aims to be a small, professional, and welcoming OSS project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Focus on code, tests, documentation, and concrete evidence.
- Give feedback on technical merit, not on the person making the contribution.
- Assume good intent unless the evidence says otherwise.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks or repeated hostile conduct.
- Deliberate disruption of discussion or review.
- Sharing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or reject contributions that
violate this policy.

## Reporting

If you need to report a conduct issue, open a private channel with the project
maintainer or the repository owner and include:

- a short description of what happened
- where it happened
- the affected people or systems
- any relevant logs, links, or screenshots
